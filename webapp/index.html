Obtaining elevation maps for additional dates

ALOS requires dated PRISM stereo scenes and separate DSM processing. Copernicus GLO-30 requires separate dated TanDEM-X surfaces for temporal comparison. DLR TanDEM-X DEM Change Maps are a possible additional source; Gurugram coverage and access have not been checked in this package.
